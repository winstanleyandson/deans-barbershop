The shop

One barber. One chair. Since 1998.

Dean started cutting hair in 1998 and spent ten years at Traditions in Mossley Hill before opening his own shop on Park Road in 2008.

It's a proper old-school barber's: you sit down, you get a good cut, you have a chat if you want one, and you leave looking sharp. No gimmicks, no music you can't talk over, and no pressure to buy anything.

Classic cuts and cut-throat shaves are the speciality, but a modern skin fade gets the same care.
